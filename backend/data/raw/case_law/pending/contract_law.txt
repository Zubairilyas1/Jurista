PUNJAB PRE-EMPTION ACT 1991

Section 13. Demand of Pre-emption (Talabs).
(1) The right of pre-emption of a person shall be extinguished unless such person makes demands of pre-emption in the following order:
(a) talab-i-muwathaba;
(b) talab-i-ishhad;
(c) talab-e-khusumat.

(2) Talab-i-Muwathaba means immediate demand by a pre-emptor in the sitting or meeting (Majlis) in which he has come to know of the sale declaring his intention to exercise the right of pre-emption.

(3) Talab-i-Ishhad means demand by establishing evidence. Where a pre-emptor has made talab-i-muwathaba under sub-section (2), he shall as soon thereafter as possible but not later than two weeks from the date of notice under Section 32, or knowledge, whichever is earlier, make talab-i-ishhad by sending a notice in writing attested by two truthful witnesses, under registered cover acknowledgment due, to the vendee, confirming his intention to exercise the right of pre-emption.

SUPREME COURT PRECEDENT: PLD 2015 SC 69
The Supreme Court held that the written notice of Talab-i-Ishhad must explicitly mention the date, time, and place of making the first demand (Talab-i-Muwathaba), and the names of the witnesses in whose presence it was made. Failure to state these exact details in the notice of Talab-i-Ishhad renders the pre-emption suit not maintainable and liable to be dismissed, as the right of pre-emption is a feeble right and requires strict compliance with procedural technicalities.

SUPREME COURT PRECEDENT: 2021 SCMR 1032
Strict compliance with Section 13 is mandatory. If the notice of Talab-i-Ishhad lacks the details of the time and place of the Talab-i-Muwathaba, the defect is fatal to the suit.

FINANCIAL DEPOSIT (Zar-e-Soem) - Section 24
Under Section 24 of the Punjab Pre-emption Act 1991, the plaintiff is required to deposit in the Court one-third of the sale price (Zar-e-Soem) of the property in cash within such period as the Court may fix, which shall not extend beyond thirty days from the filing of the suit. If the plaintiff fails to make a deposit under sub-section (1) within the period fixed by the Court, his suit shall be dismissed.
