ACT 2026
ACT X OF 2026
[First published, after having received the assent of the Governor of the Punjab, in the
- Gazette of the Punjab (Extraordinary) dated February 19, 2026.]
An 5
Act
to amend the Provincial Motor Vehicles Ordinance, 1965.
It is necessary to amend the Provincial Motor Vehicles Ordinance, 1965 (XIX of 1965) for the
purposes hereinafter appearing.
It is hereby enacted by Provincial Assembly of Punjab as follows:
1. Shorttitle and commencement.(1—) This Act may be cited as the Provincial Motor
Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2026.
(2) It shall come into force at once.
2. Amendment of section 4 of Ordinance XIX of 1965.— In the Provincial Motor
Vehicles Ordinance, 1965 (XIX of 1965), hereinafter referred to as “the Ordinance”, in section
4, in sub-section (1), in clause (i), after the semicolon at the end, the following proviso shall
be added:
“provided that a person who has attained the age of sixteen years may drive a motor
cycle up to 125 cc or an electric motor cycle having equivalent power if he holds the
juvenile driving permit issued by the licensing authority ih such manner as may be
prescribed;”.
